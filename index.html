📬 Sneaky Mail

Deliver the mail. Don't wake the dogs.

10 streets. 10 breeds. Zero barks (hopefully).

How to Play

  • Move: Arrow Keys or WASD
  • Sneak (slower, quieter): Hold Shift
  • Deliver mail: Walk up to a mailbox — it's automatic
  • Avoid: Dog vision cones. Getting spotted fills their suspicion meter — if it maxes out, you're busted!
  • Hide: Trees, hedges and shrubs block a dog's line of sight — and everything in the yard is solid, so plan your route around it
  • Goal: Deliver to every mailbox on the street without getting caught
  • Mail van: Space to get in and out — dogs can't see you inside, but you can't deliver from it either
  • Your satchel holds 5 letters. Walk back to the van to load up again
  • Horn: H, from the driver's seat — every dog in earshot breaks off and comes to investigate, which is how you pull one away from its mailbox. It wakes the nappers too
  • Pause: P   Restart level: R   Mute: M
